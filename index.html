Personal Trainer - Tracker Support

Personal Trainer - Tracker is a coaching system built around two connected apps: one for trainers and a separate app for each client.

Trainers can send workout and nutrition plans to clients, while clients can follow their programs and send workout, nutrition, calorie, and progress data back to the trainer in real time.
